# Changelog

Generated by [changie](https://changie.dev) from the fragments in `.changes/`. See `.forgejo/README.md` for how to add one.

## v0.5.0 - 2026-11-12
### Added
- Look up text selected in other apps: pick Okonomi in the text selection menu
- Learning English: a Learning setting under Settings → Dictionary turns the app around for Japanese speakers, with English words heading search results, Favourites, History and Phrases
- Okonomi in Japanese: the app follows the phone language, or pick one under Settings → Appearance → Language
### Fixed
- Kanji keep their Japanese shapes on phones not set to Japanese, instead of showing their Chinese forms

## v0.4.0 - 2026-10-08
### Added
- Furigana now sits over each kanji where its reading can be told apart, so a partial search match lights just the matching kanji
### Changed
- Settings is now split into Appearance and About
### Fixed
- The kanji details popup no longer closes when you rotate the phone

## v0.3.0 - 2026-10-07
### Added
- Open an entry in Okonomi straight from its AnkiDroid card
- Send your Favourites to AnkiDroid
- Import and export History, and swipe to remove words from it
- Tips on the empty Search tab
### Changed
- Search filters moved into a floating menu
